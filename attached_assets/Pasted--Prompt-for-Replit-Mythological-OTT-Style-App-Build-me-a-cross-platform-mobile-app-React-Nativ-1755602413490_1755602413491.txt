Landing/Home Screen

Clean, elegant UI with dark + golden-accent theme (to match devotional/mythological content).

Featured carousel/banner (highlight trending videos or festivals like Diwali, Janmashtami, Navratri).

Category sections (horizontal scrolls):

Ramayana Stories

Mahabharata Stories

Lord Krishna

Lord Shiva

Bhajans & Aartis

Explained: Religious Myths

Infinite scroll for browsing.

Video Player Page

Fullscreen player with short video playback (1–2 mins).

Like, Share, Save, and Playlist options.

Option to autoplay next video.

Categories & Discovery

Category tabs / Explore screen.

Search bar with filters (by God, Epic, Genre, Duration).

User Accounts (Basic)

Sign in via Google/Email.

Saved playlists (favourites, watch later).

History tracking.

OTT-style feel but simplified for short-form content.

Smooth animations (category transitions, hover effects).
